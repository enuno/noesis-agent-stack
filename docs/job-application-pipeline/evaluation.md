# Evaluation

## Scoring formula

Use a transparent weighted score:

fit = 0.30S + 0.25L + 0.20I + 0.15C + 0.10G

Where:
- S = technical-skill alignment
- L = leadership/scope alignment
- I = industry alignment
- C = compensation confidence
- G = geographic/remote alignment

## Scoring guidance

### Technical skill alignment (S)
Score higher when the posting maps to:
- AI agents and orchestration,
- LLM infrastructure,
- Kubernetes, Docker, Terraform, Ansible,
- network architecture,
- cloud and edge infrastructure,
- blockchain / DePIN,
- Python / Node.js,
- operational excellence and platform ownership.

### Leadership / scope alignment (L)
Score higher when the role explicitly includes:
- technical strategy,
- infrastructure ownership,
- team leadership,
- cross-functional coordination,
- architecture responsibility,
- operational excellence.

### Industry alignment (I)
Score higher when the company or team works in:
- AI infrastructure,
- agent platforms,
- blockchain infrastructure,
- DePIN,
- cybersecurity,
- edge computing,
- distributed systems.

### Compensation confidence (C)
Score higher when:
- a base range is stated,
- total compensation is stated,
- the role is clearly above the floor,
- the employer is known to compensate competitively at this level.

Salary-unlisted leadership roles should move to a verification lane, not automatically reject.

### Geographic / remote alignment (G)
Score higher when the role is:
- Remote US,
- Remote Americas,
- Denver metro / Colorado hybrid,
- or otherwise compatible with the stated geography preferences.

## Decision bands

- `pursue` — high conviction; prepare packet and queue for approval.
- `watch` — promising but not ready; revisit later.
- `needs_research` — missing comp or fit details that matter.
- `reject` — outside scope, below floor, or poor fit.

## Review checklist

Before promoting a role to `pursue`, verify:
- company canonicalized,
- requisition ID captured if present,
- job URL captured,
- source hash recorded,
- compensation policy checked,
- remote policy checked,
- no duplicate canonical record exists,
- no unsupported claims in the match summary.
